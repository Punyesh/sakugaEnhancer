KEYFRAME LOOKUP

Look up animation staff by name and see their full credit history, grouped by role — right inside your browser. No install, no account, no server.
STEP 1
Drag this to your bookmarks bar

Not click — drag it up to your browser's bookmarks bar. If your bookmarks bar is hidden, show it first (Ctrl+Shift+B in Chrome). Works the same way in Firefox and other browsers too.

📌 KeyFrame Lookup
↑ drag, don't click ↑

This bookmarklet is short on purpose — it just loads the real script fresh from GitHub each time you click it, so it always picks up updates automatically without you needing to redo anything. If dragging doesn't save it, type javascript: by hand into the bookmark's URL field first, then paste just the rest.

STEP 2
Go to the site

Open keyframe-staff-list.com and let it load normally.

STEP 3
Click your new bookmark

A small panel appears — drag it anywhere by its header. Type or paste names (one per line, or comma-separated), click Run lookup, then View Results once it's done for a full formatted page (grouped by role with cover art, click a role to expand it), or Download JSON for the raw data. There's also a Credit Sheet Organizer mode — click the link at the bottom of the panel to switch, including its own editable role dictionary if you run into an abbreviation it doesn't recognize.
